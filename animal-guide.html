Coordination / Balance

Moving the whole body in unusual patterns and staying stable on a small base. You train control, body awareness and balance — useful at any age, and more so with age.

Author's preference

Animal moves, without question. To the author, many yoga poses joined into a flowing sequence are already animal moves.

Examples
  • Animal moves: bear crawl, crab walk, ape walk, frog jumps, lizard crawl, duck walk, inchworm
  • Flowing transitions (from Animal Flow): beast hold, side kickthrough, crab reach, scorpion reach, underswitch
  • Balance: single-leg stand (then with eyes closed), tree pose, warrior III, single-leg deadlift, walking heel-to-toe along a line
Tips

Move slowly and with control first, then faster. Change direction: forward, backward, sideways. Do balance work near a wall or a support.

This is the author’s personal view, not sports or medical advice. If you have health problems or injuries, or the workouts are for a child, ask a doctor or a coach first.
