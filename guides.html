All workouts explained

Warm-up

5–10 minutes before any workout: raise the pulse, warm up the muscles, mobilise the joints.

Power

Short, fast efforts: 20–30 seconds, up to about 20 reps, with a light-to-moderate load of roughly 20–40% of your maximum. The goal is speed and explosiveness, not grinding.

Speed & Reaction

Very short, very fast efforts of 5–15 seconds with long rest. You train quick feet, quick hands and a fast response to a signal — not strength and not endurance.

Endurance

There are two kinds of endurance, and both matter: muscular endurance — the intervals in this app, and endurance in minutes — long steady activity for the heart and breathing.

Coordination / Balance

Moving the whole body in unusual patterns and staying stable on a small base. You train control, body awareness and balance — useful at any age, and more so with age.

Strength

Lifting heavy loads with good technique. Each session follows the same pattern, and the load changes from week to week: heavy weeks for maximal strength, medium weeks for strength and muscle endurance, and once a month a light “marathon” session.

Stretching

Slow, calm stretches held in place. You gradually get back range of motion in the joints, release tension after hard work and help recovery. If you feel stiff as a board, this is exactly for you — progress comes slowly, over weeks, but it comes.
