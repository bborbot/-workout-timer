Workouts

Recommended per week for a healthy adult (based on WHO guidance: 150–300 min of moderate or 75–150 min of vigorous activity, strength at least 2 days). Set your own weekly plan under each workout. A workout played in the app is marked automatically with its minutes; tap + if you trained without the app. At the start of a new week you will see how last week went. Marks are stored on this phone only.

This is the author’s personal view, not sports or medical advice. If you have health problems or injuries, or the workouts are for a child, ask a doctor or a coach first.
