Warm-up

5–10 minutes before any workout: raise the pulse, warm up the muscles, mobilise the joints.

Author's preference

Large, high-amplitude circular movements of the arms, legs and torso. Chinese health gymnastics (qigong) works very well here.

Examples
  • Arm circles forward and backward — one arm, then both
  • Shoulder rolls; slow neck half-circles from shoulder to shoulder
  • Torso circles and twists, hip circles
  • Leg swings forward–back and side to side; knee and ankle circles
  • From Ba Duan Jin (“Eight Brocades”): Holding up the Heavens, Drawing the Bow, Separating Heaven and Earth
  • Arm swinging (Shuai Shou) from qigong
  • Last 1–2 minutes: easy bouncing or high knees on the spot
Tips

Start small and slow, make each round bigger. Keep moving — no long static stretches before Power or Strength. Movement should never hurt.

This is the author’s personal view, not sports or medical advice. If you have health problems or injuries, or the workouts are for a child, ask a doctor or a coach first.
